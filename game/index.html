🔍 SPOT THE DIFFERENCE

Pasar Malam & More — two pictures, a handful of secrets

How to play. Two pictures sit side by side — they look the same, but a few things are different. Click or tap a difference on either picture to circle it. Find them all before the timer runs out.
Keyboard: arrow keys move the golden crosshair, Enter marks the spot, H asks for a hint (3 per scene, −25 points), M turns the magnifier on and off.
Scoring: +100 per find, streak and speed bonuses, −10 for a wrong click. Three stars if you beat the par time. Five scenes, 38 differences — good hunting!
